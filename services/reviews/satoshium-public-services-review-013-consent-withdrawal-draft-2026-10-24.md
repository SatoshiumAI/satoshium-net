## Proposed notice

**What this is.** You are being invited to consider an optional, short evaluation of three already-public SATOSHIUM demonstration tools. We want to learn whether their instructions, controls, results, and limitations are understandable. This is not a product certification, financial service, institutional attestation, or real-agent deployment.

**What you would do.** If you choose to participate, you may try sample tasks using the Aegis Inspection Panel, Agent Governance Tool, and Verification Ledger Tool. You can skip tasks or questions. Please use only the provided fictitious inputs. Do not enter private keys, passwords, approval tokens, sensitive personal information, real agent IDs, actual organizational policies, or information belonging to others.

**Where data goes.** Aegis sends the selected sample actor identifiers to the public Satoshium API for a read-only inspection. Agent Governance and Verification Ledger generate and check demonstration records locally in your browser. These pages load Cloudflare Web Analytics for site-level pageview/referral measurement, request a shared footer from satoshium.link, and make requests to satoshium.ai for site assets or linked content. A browser network-origin check observed these domains; the exact request details, hosting logs and retention have not been independently established. Visiting these pages therefore involves separate network requests to third-party or other Satoshium-managed hosts; server-side logging and their data practices have not been independently verified for this draft. The feedback described here would be collected separately using **[APPROVED FEEDBACK CHANNEL — NOT SELECTED]**. Do not assume page analytics establish whether you participated or completed tasks. This notice does not claim to describe all possible infrastructure logs or third-party data practices; those must be reviewed before invitations.

**What we would record.** With your agreement, a facilitator may record a pseudonymous session code; whether a task was completed, skipped, or required help; whether results and service boundaries were understood; and brief sanitized improvement suggestions. No video, audio, screen recording, raw exported records, identifying contact details, or screenshots would be collected by default. **[OPTIONAL RECORDING OR OTHER DATA PROCESSES MUST BE SEPARATELY DISCLOSED AND APPROVED; NONE ARE PROPOSED HERE.]**

**Important limitations.** Aegis does not activate or certify agents. A Governance result labeled APPROVED is a local reference-evaluator outcome, not execution permission. A successful SHA-256 comparison checks internal record consistency, not who created the record. Verification-ready TRUE is not evidence of Bitcoin anchoring or a completed transaction.

**Your choice.** Participation is voluntary. You may decline, skip a step, or stop at any time without penalty. You may ask questions before deciding. If you wish to request removal of your identifiable or code-linked feedback while it remains retrievable, contact **[APPROVED PILOT CONTACT — NOT SELECTED]** and provide your session code, if available. Once notes have been irreversibly aggregated or de-identified, a specific contribution may not be locatable for removal. Withdrawing from this optional study does not automatically erase standard third-party website analytics or technical logs; any applicable requests or rights need a separately approved process.

**Risks and assistance.** The tools are public demonstrations. A privacy risk could arise if you enter real confidential data by mistake; do not do so. If you have accessibility needs, contact **[APPROVED ACCESSIBILITY CONTACT — NOT SELECTED]** before participation so appropriate alternatives can be considered. No representation of full accessibility certification is made; Review 011 manual native zoom and screen-reader checks remain open.

**How feedback would be stored.** Access, storage location, retention and deletion would follow an approved minimal-data specification shared before you choose to participate: **[STORAGE / RETENTION / ACCESS DETAILS — NOT YET APPROVED]**. No participant-feedback collection should begin until these are filled in.
