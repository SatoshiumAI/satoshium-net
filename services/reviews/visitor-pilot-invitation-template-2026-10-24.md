## Six neutral prompts for consenting testers

1. Which service did you try?
2. Could you complete the example without assistance? Yes / Partly / No / Not attempted.
3. What did the tool do, in your own words?
4. Where did you hesitate or get stuck?
5. How useful was it for your work or research (1–5, optional)?
6. Would you return or recommend it? Yes / Maybe / No; why?

**Private feedback rule:** Only de-identified, consented summaries belong in public GitHub. Retain contact information, verbatim replies and identifiable screenshots outside the public repository.
