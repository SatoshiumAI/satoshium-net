Aegis: The Agent Firewall

Aegis is Satoshium's AI agent inspection and lifecycle verification service for reviewing sample autonomous-agent states, structured readiness outputs, containment posture, service boundaries, and exportable verification artifacts.

Lifecycle Intelligence Tracks stability posture, transitions, lifecycle states, and readiness signals across sample agent profiles.
Inspection Readiness Produces structured review signals for lifecycle inspection, documentation, screenshots, and technical evaluation.
Exportable Bundle Surfaces Supports manifest descriptors, archive inspection, deterministic hashing, and artifact-class export identity.
Verification Review Surfaces Exposes compatibility preview structures for documentation and verification review without performing external writes.

Included capabilities

  • Lifecycle stability evaluation with transition and persistence detection.
  • Inspection readiness signals for lifecycle review, documentation, and technical evaluation.
  • Service compatibility projections suitable for review-safe evaluation workflows.
