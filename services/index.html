Aegis: The Agent Firewall

Aegis is Satoshium’s lifecycle verification engine for evaluating whether autonomous agents remain safe, unstable, or hostile across time. It exposes structured readiness signals for registry participation, coordination routing, bundle export identity, and future certification surfaces across the platform’s trust layer.

Lifecycle Intelligence Tracks stability posture, transitions, and long-horizon readiness signals across agent containment lifecycles.
Registry & Coordination Readiness Produces deterministic participation and routing signals for downstream indexing, snapshotting, and coordination modules.
Exportable Bundle Surfaces Supports manifest descriptors, archive inspection, deterministic hashing, and artifact-class export identity.
Chronicle & Anchor Compatibility Exposes preview-level handshake readiness for Chronicle logging and future Anchor commitment layers without performing external writes.

Included capabilities

  • Lifecycle stability evaluation with transition and persistence detection.
  • Registry participation readiness signals for indexing, coordination, and snapshot surfaces.
  • Certification-surface compatibility projections suitable for future Certifier interoperability.
  • Bundle export infrastructure including manifests, readiness descriptors, ZIP schema, and deterministic hash identity.
  • Chronicle handshake and Anchor preview compatibility for future proof-chain integration.

Aegis is published as an inspectable lifecycle-verification subsystem within Satoshium’s trust-layer architecture, with machine-readable readiness signals and canonical bundle access for experimentation and review.

Researchers, developers, and infrastructure teams exploring agent governance and coordination systems are invited to inspect the subsystem and share feedback.
