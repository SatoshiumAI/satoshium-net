The Satoshium Domain Architecture

Satoshium Net is one layer within a broader multi-domain ecosystem. Each domain has a distinct role, allowing the project to separate public presence, experiments, infrastructure, knowledge, and commerce.

  • satoshium.ai — public-facing platform and primary brand surface
  • satoshium-progress — public build surface and visible development progress
  • satoshium.info — knowledge layer, documentation hub, and engineering surface
  • satoshium.xyz — experimental systems, agent testing, and frontier concepts
  • satoshium.net — network, infrastructure, and future service visibility
  • satoshium.store — merchandise and economic/brand layer
Together, these domains form a layered ecosystem rather than a single flat website.
