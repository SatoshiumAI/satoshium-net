Satoshium Signal Boards

Inspired by the early forums and mailing lists that shaped Bitcoin’s emergence, Signal Boards provide a public discussion layer for long-horizon thinking across Satoshium, Bitcoin infrastructure, and constitutional AI systems.

Satoshium Board

Platform architecture, Canon development, verification infrastructure, governance tools, simulations, and ecosystem roadmap discussion.

Bitcoin Board

Base-layer trust systems, Lightning identity, sovereign infrastructure, treasury adoption, and long-term monetary transition signals.

AI Alignment Board

Agent governance, explainability, verification frameworks, constitutional AI models, and cryptographic automation research.
