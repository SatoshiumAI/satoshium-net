# Submit a Signal Proposal

[Signal Submission • Curated Intake Layer]

Signal proposals help improve the long-horizon development of the Satoshium platform.

Submissions are reviewed before any public posting and may be edited for clarity, alignment, or structure.

Signal submission intake is currently curator-reviewed prior to publication.

---

## Purpose of Signal Submissions

Signal proposals allow contributors to:

- suggest new infrastructure coordination ideas
- propose architectural directions
- introduce verification-aware frameworks
- document emerging governance considerations
- expand simulation-informed development pathways
- strengthen alignment between Canon, governance, and automation layers

Signals are preserved as part of the coordination memory of the Satoshium network.

---

## Submission Requirements

Signal proposals should include:

- a clear signal title
- a relevant Signal Board selection
- a structured proposal description
- alignment with participation guidelines

Optional fields may include:

- contributor name or alias
- contact email

Participation guidelines must be acknowledged prior to submission.

---

## Relevant Signal Boards

Signals may be submitted to:

- Satoshium Board
- Bitcoin Board
- AI Alignment Board

Each board preserves reasoning context for its respective coordination domain.

---

## Review Process

Submitted signals are:

- reviewed prior to publication
- evaluated for clarity and alignment
- structured for archival continuity if accepted

Signals may be:

- approved
- edited
- deferred
- declined

Approval is not automatic.
