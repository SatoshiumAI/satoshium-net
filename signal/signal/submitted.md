## What Happens Next

After submission, signals enter the curated review process.

During review, proposals may be:

- approved for publication
- edited for clarity or structure
- deferred for later consideration
- declined if misaligned with Signal Board scope

Approval is not automatic.

---

## Role of the Review Process

The review process helps preserve:

- signal clarity
- infrastructure alignment
- long-horizon architectural continuity
- verification-aware coordination standards

Signal Boards function as an archival discussion layer rather than an open forum.
