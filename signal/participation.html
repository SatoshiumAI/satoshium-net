Signal Board Participation

Signal Boards operate as an archival discussion layer within the Satoshium network. Participation is currently curated and reviewed before any public posting is approved.

How participation works

At this stage, Signal Board entries are not open-posted. Proposed signals, thread ideas, and discussion submissions are reviewed before appearing publicly.

What Signal Boards are for

  • Constructive discussion
  • Technical and architectural ideas
  • Verification-minded analysis
  • Long-horizon thinking
  • Respectful public signal

What may be declined

  • Harsh, rude, or hostile language
  • Personal attacks
  • Speculation presented as fact
  • Market hype or spam-style promotion
  • Off-topic submissions
  • Unverified accusations or inflammatory content

Moderation approach

Signal Boards are moderated to preserve clarity, civility, and trust. Approval is not automatic. Submissions may be edited, deferred, or declined if they do not align with the tone and purpose of the Signal Boards.

Current phase

Phase 1 participation is curator-reviewed. Expanded participation methods may be introduced in future releases.
