Signals for Bitcoin-Aligned Infrastructure and Coordination Systems

[OPEN THREAD] Participation Layer

This thread invites structured ideas that may strengthen the infrastructure, coordination, and long-horizon utility of Bitcoin-aligned systems. Relevant signals may include proposals related to nodes, mining, treasury infrastructure, Lightning coordination, Layer-2 environments, verification tooling, and other open systems that build in alignment with Bitcoin as a trust base layer.

This is not a thread for general market commentary or short-cycle speculation. Signals are most useful when they contribute to durable infrastructure thinking, clear technical direction, or improved coordination pathways across Bitcoin-native or Bitcoin-aligned environments.

During the current phase, submissions are reviewed before any public posting. Future participation layers may support structured signal proposals, verified contributors, or additional submission and review pathways.

Signals that improve verifiability, resilience, interoperability, or long-horizon infrastructure design are especially encouraged.
