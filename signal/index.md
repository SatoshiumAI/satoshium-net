# 📡 Satoshium Signal Boards

Signal Boards provide a public discussion layer for long-horizon coordination across Satoshium, Bitcoin infrastructure, and constitutional AI systems.

Inspired by the early forums and mailing lists that shaped Bitcoin’s emergence, Signal Boards operate as an archival signaling surface within the Satoshium network architecture.

---

# 🧭 Purpose

Signal Boards exist to support structured visibility of:

- platform architecture evolution
- infrastructure direction signals
- governance model discussion
- registry-aligned system changes
- decentralized intelligence research pathways
- long-horizon coordination proposals

They function as coordination infrastructure rather than conversational forums.

---

# 🧱 Signal Board Structure

Signal Boards are organized into three primary coordination surfaces.

Signal Boards currently operate in archival mode.

This means they:

- preserve coordination signals
- document long-horizon architectural thinking
- maintain infrastructure context
- support traceability of platform evolution

Expanded participation layers may be introduced in future releases.

# 🛡️ Governance Compatibility

Signal Boards support governance-aware coordination by:

- documenting infrastructure intent
- preserving proposal context
- tracking system evolution signals
- supporting registry-aligned discussion pathways

They do not function as voting systems or decision authorities.
