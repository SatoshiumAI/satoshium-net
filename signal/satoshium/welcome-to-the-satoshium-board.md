# Welcome to the Satoshium Board

[Pinned Signal • Orientation Layer]

The Satoshium Signal Board serves as an archival discussion layer for long-horizon thinking about decentralized intelligence infrastructure. It documents ideas, structures, and signals that shape the evolution of the Satoshium platform.

Unlike traditional forums, Signal Boards are not designed for rapid conversation. They are designed for clarity, durability, and architectural context. Entries are curated to preserve alignment between governance, verification systems, simulation environments, and constitutional AI development.

Signals recorded here help explain how components of the Satoshium network relate to one another over time. They provide a visible record of infrastructure direction rather than a stream of short-lived commentary.

Future participation layers may allow structured signal proposals, signed entries, and verification-linked contributions. During the current phase, signals are curated to establish the foundational structure of the platform.
