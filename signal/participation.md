# Signal Board Participation

Signal Boards operate as an archival discussion layer within the Satoshium network.

Participation is currently curated and reviewed before any public posting is approved.

---

## How Participation Works

At this stage, Signal Board entries are not open-posted. Proposed signals, thread ideas, and discussion submissions are reviewed before appearing publicly.

Participation is curator-reviewed to ensure alignment with the long-horizon infrastructure purpose of Signal Boards.

---

## What Signal Boards Are For

Signal Boards support:

- constructive discussion
- technical and architectural ideas
- verification-minded analysis
- long-horizon thinking
- respectful public signal

Signals recorded here contribute to the coordination memory of the Satoshium platform.

---

## What May Be Declined

The following types of submissions may be declined:

- harsh, rude, or hostile language
- personal attacks
- speculation presented as fact
- market hype or promotional spam
- off-topic submissions
- unverified accusations
- inflammatory content

These guidelines help preserve clarity, credibility, and signal integrity.

---

## Moderation Approach

Signal Boards are moderated to preserve:

- clarity
- civility
- infrastructure alignment
- trust-aware coordination tone

Approval is not automatic. Submissions may be edited, deferred, or declined if they do not align with the purpose of the Signal Boards.

---

## Current Phase

Phase 1 participation is curator-reviewed.

Expanded participation methods may be introduced in future releases, including structured signal workflows and verification-linked coordination pathways.
